# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Professionals who handle many files a day: designers exporting PDFs and images, video editors handing off renders,
developers shipping assets. They work on macOS, Windows or Linux, usually with several other apps open, and reach for
FileForge between tasks: drop a batch of files, pick how hard to squeeze, check the numbers, save the results where they
belong, get back to the main app. They know what DPI, JPEG quality and CRF mean and want to see and control them.

## Product Purpose
A local desktop tool that makes files smaller and changes their formats (documents, images, video, audio) without
uploading anything anywhere. The first shipped tool is PDF compression. Success: a professional trusts the output
enough to send it without re-checking, because the app shows exactly what it changed and never makes a file worse
or bigger.

## Brand Personality
Precise, calm, trustworthy. A native utility in the spirit of ImageOptim and Raycast: quiet chrome, clear numbers,
no marketing voice inside the app. The four sheet colors of the app icon (blue, green, orange, red) are the only
expressive element; they identify the file-type groups and nothing else.

## Anti-references
- Online "compress PDF free" sites: ad-heavy, upload-first, vague promises, progress theatre.
- Glossy skeuomorphic UI copied from the icon; the icon is the only glossy thing.
- SaaS dashboard look: hero metrics, gradient accents, identical card grids.
- Modal-driven flows for things that fit inline.

## Design Principles
1. Numbers over adjectives: show sizes, ratios, DPI and quality values, not "optimized!".
2. Safe by default: lossless is the default, originals are never touched, a result is never larger than its input.
3. Batch-first: every tool works on a list of files; one file is just a short list.
4. Disappear into the task: drop, adjust, save; no wizards, no onboarding tours.
5. Native on every OS: respects system theme, language and window conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, visible keyboard focus, full keyboard operation, reduced-motion alternatives.
Group colors are never the only carrier of meaning (always paired with a label or icon). Interface in English and
Russian, following the system language with a manual switch.
